# Lumen Points Loyalty Program

Lumen Points reward you for shopping with us. They are free to join and are added to your account automatically.

## How to earn points

You earn 1 point for every $1 spent, before tax and shipping. Writing a review earns 25 points, and referring a friend earns 200 points once their first order ships. Points are added to your account 14 days after delivery, so that returned items do not earn points.

## Membership tiers

There are three tiers, based on the points earned in the last 12 months. Silver starts at 0 points, Gold starts at 1,000 points, and Platinum starts at 3,000 points. Your tier is checked every month, and you keep it for at least 12 months after you reach it.

## Tier benefits

Silver members get early access to sales. Gold members also earn 1.25 points per dollar and get free express shipping on orders over $150. Platinum members earn 1.5 points per dollar, get free express shipping on every order, and have a priority support line.

## Redeeming points

Every 100 points are worth $5 at checkout. You can use points on any order, but you cannot use them on gift cards. Points cannot be combined with a discount code or a price match on the same item.

## When points expire

Points expire 18 months after you earn them, oldest first. We send a reminder by email 30 days before any points expire. Spending or earning points does not extend the date of older points.

## Points on returns

If you return an item, the points earned on it are removed. If you already spent those points, the amount is taken from your next points earned. Points used to pay for a returned item are returned to your account.

## Referral rewards

Share your referral link from the Account page. Your friend gets 15% off their first order with the code REFER15, and you get 200 points when their order ships. You can refer up to 20 friends a year.

## Birthday reward

Gold and Platinum members get 150 bonus points in the month of their birthday. Add your birthday in the Account settings at least 30 days ahead.

## Ending your membership

Closing your account ends your membership, and unused points are cancelled. Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred to another account.
