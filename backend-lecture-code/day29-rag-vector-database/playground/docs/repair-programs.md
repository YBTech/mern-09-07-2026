# Free Repair Programs

Some products have a known fault that we fix for free, even after the warranty ends. Quote the program number when you contact support. Each program applies only to the models and production dates listed below.

## How the programs work

If your product is part of a program, we pay shipping both ways and fix or replace it at no charge. The repair does not extend your normal warranty. Programs stay open until the end date shown, and a claim sent on or before that date is honored even if the repair happens later.

## Finding your serial number

The serial number is printed inside the left ear cup of headphones, on the underside of speakers, and on the inside of the charging case lid for earphones. The production date is encoded in the number: the first four digits are the year and the month.

## Program LR-2291

Aria 300 headphones made between January and March 2026 can develop a loose charging port. We repair the port for free until December 2027.

## Program LR-3307

Nova speakers can crackle at high volume. We replace the speaker for free until June 2027.

## Program LR-4415

Aria 500 headphones can lose Bluetooth connection after a firmware update. We replace the headphones for free until March 2028.

## Program LR-5120

Pulse earphones can stop charging in their case. We replace the case for free until September 2027.

## Program LR-6034

Nova Mini speakers can switch off when the battery is below 20%. We replace the battery for free until January 2028.

## Program LR-7188

Aria 300 headphones can develop a squeak in the headband hinge. We replace the hinge for free until August 2027.

## Program LR-7420

Pulse earphones can lose sound in the right earpiece after a firmware update. We update the firmware and replace the earpiece if needed, for free, until November 2027.

## Program LR-8056

Travel Cases with the gray zipper can split along the seam. We send a replacement case for free until April 2028.

## Eligibility and limits

A product that was damaged by water or a drop is not eligible, even if it belongs to a program. Each product can be repaired under a program once. If the same fault returns, we replace the product under the standard warranty.
