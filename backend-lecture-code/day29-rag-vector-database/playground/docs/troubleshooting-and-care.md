# Troubleshooting and Care

Most problems with headphones and speakers can be fixed in a few minutes. This guide covers the most common ones, and how to look after your product.

## Pairing over Bluetooth

Turn the product on and hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue. Open the Bluetooth settings on your phone and choose the Lumen name from the list. If the product does not appear, turn Bluetooth off and on again, and move closer than one meter.

## Forgetting an old pairing

If a product connects to the wrong device, hold the power and volume-down buttons for 10 seconds. The light flashes red and the pairing list is cleared. Remove the Lumen name from your phone's Bluetooth list too, then pair again.

## No sound in one ear

First check the balance setting on your phone, which can move all the sound to one side. Then clean the ear tip, because earwax and dust can block the sound. If one side is still silent after a firmware update and a reset, the product may qualify for a repair program or the warranty.

## Charging problems

Use the cable from the box and a charger of at least 5 watts. Check that the charging port has no lint, and clean it with a dry wooden toothpick. A full charge takes about 2 hours. If the light does not turn on after 30 minutes, try a different cable.

## Firmware updates

The Lumen app tells you when an update is available. Keep the product on the charger, and within 3 meters of your phone, until the update finishes, which takes about 10 minutes. Do not turn the product off during an update.

## How to reset your headphones, earphones, or speakers

To reset your headphones, earphones, or speaker to factory settings, put it in its case or on the charger and hold the power button for 15 seconds, until the light blinks white twice. A reset clears all pairings and settings, but it does not change the firmware. Try a reset first when a product freezes, will not connect, or behaves strangely.

## Cleaning ear tips and cushions

Remove the ear tips and wash them in warm water with mild soap. Dry them fully before putting them on again. Wipe the ear cushions with a slightly damp cloth. Never use alcohol or bleach, because they dry out the foam.

## Sweat and water resistance

Pulse earphones have an IPX4 rating, which handles sweat and light rain. Aria headphones are not water resistant. Nova speakers have an IPX7 rating and can be dropped in shallow water for up to 30 minutes. Water resistance is not covered by the warranty if the product has been damaged first.

## Battery care

Batteries last longest when kept between 20% and 80%. Do not leave the product in a hot car, because heat shortens battery life. If you will not use it for months, charge it to about 50% and store it somewhere cool and dry.

## Microphone and call quality

If people cannot hear you on calls, check that the product is selected as the input device on your phone or computer. Make sure nothing covers the microphone openings, and try a call in a quieter spot, because wind noise can overwhelm the microphone.

## Noise cancelling tips

Noise cancelling works best with a tight seal. Try a different ear tip size, and check that the cushions sit flat against your head, with no hair or glasses frames in between. Turn on the app's fit test to see how good the seal is.
