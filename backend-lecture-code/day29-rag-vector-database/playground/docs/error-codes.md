# Error Codes

Lumen Audio products show an error code with a flashing light pattern, and in the Lumen app. This page lists every code, what it means, and the first thing to try. If the first fix does not work, contact support and quote the code.

## How to read an error code

The light flashes amber, then red once for each digit of the code after E-40. The Lumen app shows the full code under Settings, then Product health. Write down the code before you restart the product, because restarting clears it from the light.

## Error E-4001

The Bluetooth radio fails its start-up check. This is reported as error E-4001. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to restart the product. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4001.

## Error E-4002

The charging controller reports a voltage fault. This is reported as error E-4002. Unplug the cable, wait 30 seconds, and plug it in again. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4002.

## Error E-4003

The battery sensor cannot read the battery level. This is reported as error E-4003. Charge the product for 2 hours, then restart it. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4003.

## Error E-4004

The microphone fails its self-test. This is reported as error E-4004. Restart the product and make sure nothing covers the microphone openings. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4004.

## Error E-4005

The touch panel stops responding to taps. This is reported as error E-4005. Wipe the touch area with a dry cloth, then restart the product. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4005.

## Error E-4006

The noise-cancelling processor fails its start-up check. This is reported as error E-4006. Turn noise cancelling off and on again from the Lumen app. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4006.

## Error E-4007

The left speaker driver reports a signal fault. This is reported as error E-4007. Restart the product and test it with a different audio source. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4007.

## Error E-4008

The right speaker driver reports a signal fault. This is reported as error E-4008. Restart the product and test it with a different audio source. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4008.

## Error E-4009

The memory fails its integrity check. This is reported as error E-4009. Reset the product to factory settings and pair it again. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4009.

## Error E-4010

The firmware update stops before it finishes. This is reported as error E-4010. Keep the product on the charger and run the update again from the Lumen app. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4010.

## Error E-4011

The temperature sensor reports a reading outside the safe range. This is reported as error E-4011. Let the product cool down or warm up to room temperature for 30 minutes. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4011.

## Error E-4012

The pairing module times out while connecting to a phone. This is reported as error E-4012. Remove the product from the Bluetooth list on your phone, then pair it again. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4012.

## Error E-4013

The charging case contact cannot detect the earphones. This is reported as error E-4013. Clean the metal contacts in the case with a dry cotton swab. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4013.

## Error E-4014

The wear sensor does not detect when the product is worn. This is reported as error E-4014. Clean the sensor window and check that the ear tips fit snugly. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4014.

## Error E-4015

The volume control reports a stuck button. This is reported as error E-4015. Press each volume button 10 times, then restart the product. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4015.

## Error E-4016

The power button reports a stuck button. This is reported as error E-4016. Press the power button 10 times, then restart the product. If the error appears again after the fix, contact support and quote E-4016.

## When to contact support

If an error returns twice in one week, or if more than one error code appears at the same time, contact support. The product may qualify for the warranty or for one of the free repair programs.
