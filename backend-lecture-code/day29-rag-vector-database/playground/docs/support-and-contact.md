# Support and Contact

This page explains how to reach Lumen Audio, how fast we reply, and how to escalate a problem.

## Support hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, from 8 am to 8 pm Eastern, and on Saturday from 10 am to 4 pm Eastern. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays in Ontario. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

## Ways to contact us

You can start a chat from the Help button on our site, email support, or request a call. Chat is the fastest, with a typical wait of under 5 minutes. Email replies arrive within one business day. Phone calls are by appointment so that we can have your order ready.

## What to have ready

Please have your order number, the product name, and the serial number if you have one. For a fault, a short video of the problem helps a lot. Do not send card numbers or passwords, because we will never ask for them.

## Response times

We aim to reply to every message within one business day. Warranty claims and refund requests are answered within 2 business days. During sale events the wait can be longer, and we post the current wait time on the Help page.

## Languages

We support customers in English and French. Chat and email are available in both. Phone calls in French are available on weekdays between 9 am and 5 pm Eastern.

## Escalating a problem

If a case is not resolved after two replies, ask for a team lead and we will review it within one business day. You can also write to the customer care manager, whose address is on the Help page. We keep a record of the case, so you do not need to explain it again.

## Accessibility

Our website works with screen readers and with keyboard-only navigation, and every product video has captions. If you cannot use a part of the site, contact us and we will help you place the order by chat, email, or phone at no extra cost.

## Feedback and complaints

Tell us what we can do better from the feedback form on the Help page. Formal complaints receive a written response within 15 business days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may contact your provincial consumer protection office.

## Press and business enquiries

Journalists and partners can reach our communications team through the Press page. Business orders of 10 or more units are handled by our business team, who reply within one business day with a quote.
