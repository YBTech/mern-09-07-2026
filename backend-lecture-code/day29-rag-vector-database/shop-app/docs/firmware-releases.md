# Firmware Releases

Firmware updates add features and fix faults. The Lumen app tells you when a new version is ready. This page lists each release and what changed.

## How to check your version

Open the Lumen app, choose your product, and select About. The firmware version is shown as a number such as 2.4.1. Keep the product on the charger while it updates.

## Firmware 2.1.0

Released in January 2026. Adds multipoint pairing, so the product can stay connected to two devices at the same time.

## Firmware 2.2.0

Released in March 2026. Improves noise cancelling in windy places and adds a wind-noise setting in the Lumen app.

## Firmware 2.3.0

Released in May 2026. Adds an equalizer with five presets and a custom mode.

## Firmware 2.4.0

Released in July 2026. Adds auto-pause when the product is taken off, and a faster pairing mode.

## Firmware 2.4.1

Released in August 2026. Fixes the Bluetooth drop that some Aria 500 owners saw after update 2.4.0, and fixes a crackle at high volume on Nova speakers.

## Firmware 2.5.0

Released in October 2026. Adds spatial audio for supported phones and reduces standby battery use by about 20%.

## Going to an older version

We do not offer downgrades. If a new version causes a problem, contact support and quote your firmware version, and we will tell you if a fix is on the way.
