# Product SKU Guide

Every Lumen Audio product has a SKU, a code that says which model, color, and plug it is. The SKU is printed on the box and on the order confirmation. This page lists the SKUs of our current headphones and earphones.

## How to read a SKU

A SKU has three parts. The first part is the model: LA3 is the Aria 300, LA5 is the Aria 500, and LP2 is the Pulse. The second part is the color. The third part is the region of the plug: EU or US.

## Finding the SKU of a product you own

The SKU is on the label of the original box and in the order confirmation email. If you no longer have either, the serial number inside the product tells support which model and color you have.
