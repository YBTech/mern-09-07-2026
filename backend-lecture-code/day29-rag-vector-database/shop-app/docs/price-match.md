# Price Match

If you find a lower price for the same product, we may match it. This page explains which stores and prices qualify.

## Which stores qualify

We match the price of the same product sold new by Best Buy, Amazon (sold by Amazon, not third-party sellers), and Walmart. The product must be the same model and color, and in stock at that store. The store must ship to a Canadian address.

## Time window

A price match can be requested before purchase, or within 7 days after delivery. If the price drops within those 7 days, we refund the difference. After 7 days, a price drop is not eligible.

## How to request a price match

Contact support with a link to the lower price. For a request after purchase, include your order number. We reply within one business day, and an approved difference is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Price drops on our own site

If the price of a product falls on our own site within 14 days of delivery, we refund the difference. This price protection is automatic after you ask for it, and it does not apply to flash events or clearance items.

## Exclusions

We do not match prices from clearance sales, marketplace sellers, membership-only prices, or Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals. A price match cannot be combined with a discount code. We also do not match bundles, open-box products, or prices that need a trade-in.

## Matching bundles

When a competitor sells a headphone with a free accessory, we match only the headphone price and add a case at no charge if the accessory is one we also sell. We cannot match a free gift card or a free subscription.

## Proof of the price

A screenshot is not enough. We need a working link, or a printed flyer with the date, so that we can see the price and the stock status ourselves. If the link no longer shows the price, the request is declined.

## Currency and tax

Prices are compared in Canadian dollars before tax and shipping. A price in another currency is converted using the bank rate on the day of the request. Shipping costs are not matched.
