# Warranty

Every Lumen Audio product comes with a limited warranty against manufacturing faults. This page explains what is covered, what is not, and how to make a claim.

## Coverage period

Headphones and speakers are covered for 1 year from the delivery date. Cables, cases, and ear tips are covered for 90 days. Refurbished products carry a 6-month warranty, which is stated on the product page.

## What is covered

The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship that appear during normal use. Examples are a speaker that stops working, a charging port that fails, a headphone driver that crackles at normal volume, or buttons that stop responding. If the product cannot be fixed, we replace it with the same model or the closest current one.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover water damage, drops, cracked housings, battery wear after 300 charge cycles, or damage from unofficial repairs. Cosmetic wear such as scuffs and worn ear cushions is not covered. Products used for commercial purposes, such as in a store or studio, have a 90-day warranty instead.

## Battery wear

All rechargeable batteries lose capacity over time. We consider a battery faulty only if it holds less than 70% of its original capacity within the first 300 charge cycles. Our companion app shows the cycle count and an estimate of the remaining capacity.

## Making a claim

Send a claim from the Orders page with a short description and a video of the fault. Our team replies within 2 business days. If the claim is approved, we send a prepaid shipping label. Please do not open the product or attempt a repair, because that voids the warranty.

## Repair or replace

We decide whether to repair or replace a product. Repairs usually take 5 to 7 business days once the product reaches our workshop. If a repair would take longer than 15 business days, we offer a replacement instead. Repaired and replaced items ship to you within 10 business days of arrival at our warehouse.

## Warranty shipping costs

We pay shipping both ways for approved claims. If a claim is rejected, the product is shipped to you at no charge, and you can ask for it to be repaired for a fee. The fee is quoted before any work starts.

## Lumen Care extended plan

Lumen Care adds 2 more years of coverage and one accidental damage repair, including drops and water. It costs $29 and must be bought within 30 days of delivery. The accidental damage repair has a $25 deductible. Lumen Care cannot be bought for refurbished products.

## Transferring the warranty

The warranty belongs to the product, not to the buyer. If you give or sell the product to someone else, the remaining warranty goes with it. The new owner needs the order number, which we can look up from the serial number.

## Registering your product

Registration is not required, and your warranty starts on the delivery date either way. Registering your serial number in your account makes claims faster, because we can confirm the manufacturing date right away.
