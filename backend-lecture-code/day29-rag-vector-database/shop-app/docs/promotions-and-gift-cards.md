# Promotions and Gift Cards

This page explains how discount codes work, which codes are active, and how gift cards are bought and used.

## How discount codes work

Enter a discount code at checkout. Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied to items already in a sale. Codes apply to the item price, not to shipping or tax. If an order is partly returned, the discount is reduced in proportion to the items kept.

## WELCOME10

WELCOME10 gives 10% off a first order. It works once per account and only on orders over $50. It is added to the account automatically when you subscribe to the newsletter, and it expires 30 days after sign-up.

## STUDENT15

STUDENT15 gives 15% off for verified students. Verification is done through a student email address and lasts for 12 months. After 12 months, you verify again to keep the discount.

## BUNDLE20

BUNDLE20 gives 20% off when headphones and a case are bought together in the same order. The discount applies to the case only. The headphones and the case must be in the cart at the same time.

## FLASH40

FLASH40 gives 40% off selected speakers during the 48-hour summer flash event. It also works on clearance items, which is the only exception to the sale rule. The event is announced by email 24 hours before it starts, and stock is limited.

## Stacking rules

Discount codes cannot be combined with each other, with a price match, or with loyalty point redemptions on the same item. A sale price, a code, and free shipping can all apply to one order, as long as the code is not used on a sale item.

## Newsletter and seasonal offers

Subscribers get early access to sales and one offer a month. Seasonal events such as Black Friday have their own terms, which are published on the event page and override this page for the length of the event.

## Buying gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts from $25 to $500 and never expire. They are delivered by email within one hour, or printed and mailed for $4. A gift card cannot be used to buy another gift card.

## Using a gift card

Enter the 16-character gift card number at checkout. A gift card can be combined with a discount code and with a credit card, and any balance left on the card stays on the card. You can check the balance on the Gift Cards page.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash. A lost gift card can be replaced only with the original receipt, and we cancel the old number first. We are not able to recover a gift card number that was shared by mistake after it has been used.

## Birthday offer

Account holders who add their birthday receive a one-time 10% code in the month of their birthday. The code works on one order and cannot be combined with other codes.
