# Shipping Policy

Lumen Audio ships from one warehouse in Toronto, Canada. This policy covers delivery times, costs, international orders, and what happens when a parcel is late or lost.

## Order cutoff

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, or on a weekend or public holiday, ship on the next business day. We do not ship on weekends.

## Delivery times

Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days within Canada and 4 to 7 business days to the United States. Remote areas, such as northern Canada, can add up to 3 extra business days. The delivery estimate at checkout is the most accurate one for your address.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping costs $8.99. Express shipping costs $19.99 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days within Canada. Orders over $75 ship for free with standard shipping. The free-shipping threshold is checked after discount codes are applied.

## Express shipping

Express orders placed before 12 pm Eastern ship the same day and arrive the next business day in most Canadian cities. Express is not available to the United States, the United Kingdom, or to PO boxes. It cannot be upgraded after the order has shipped.

## International orders

We ship to Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom only. Orders to the United Kingdom take 7 to 12 business days. We cannot ship to other countries or to freight forwarders, even if the forwarder has an address in a supported country.

## Customs and import duties

Orders to the United States are shipped duty-paid when the order total is under $800. For the United Kingdom, import duties and VAT are paid by the customer on delivery. Duties are not refunded unless the whole order is returned for a refund.

## Tracking your order

You receive a tracking link by email when the order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the carrier scans the parcel. Our support team can see the same tracking information you see, so please check the link first.

## Delivery attempts

The carrier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. Parcels over $200 require a signature. If no one is available, the carrier leaves a notice and holds the parcel for 10 days at a local pickup point before it is returned to us.

## Damaged in transit

If the box is badly damaged when it arrives, take photos before you open it and contact support within 7 days. A parcel that is damaged in transit is handled as a damaged item under the refund policy, and you pay nothing for return shipping.

## Lost parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, contact support. We open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or a refund once the claim is approved, usually within 5 business days. A parcel marked delivered that you cannot find is treated the same way after a 48-hour wait for neighbors or building staff to hand it over.

## Changing the address

The shipping address can be changed until the order ships. After that, the carrier must be contacted directly, and carrier fees may apply. We cannot reroute a parcel once it is on a truck.

## Holiday shipping

During the winter holiday season, carriers are slower and our cutoff for guaranteed delivery before December 24 is December 15 for standard shipping and December 20 for express. Orders placed after those dates still ship, but we cannot promise the date.
