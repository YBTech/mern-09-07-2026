# Trade-In and Recycling

Do not throw away old audio gear. Lumen Audio accepts used headphones and speakers for trade-in or recycling.

## What can be traded in

We accept working headphones, earphones, and speakers from any brand, as long as they power on and hold a charge. Products with cracked housings, missing parts, or water damage are accepted for recycling only.

## How to get a quote

Open the Trade-In page, choose the brand and model, and answer a few questions about the condition. You get an estimated value straight away. The estimate is valid for 14 days, and the final value is confirmed after we inspect the product.

## Condition grades

We grade each product A, B, or C. Grade A works like new with light wear. Grade B has visible scratches but works fully. Grade C has a fault, such as a weak battery, but still powers on. The value of grade C is about a third of grade A.

## Sending your product

Print the free label from your quote, pack the product in any box, and drop it at a carrier location. We inspect it within 3 business days of arrival. Please remove ear tips you want to keep, and sign out of any apps or accounts linked to the product.

## How you are paid

Trade-in value is paid as Lumen store credit, which is added to your account within 2 business days after the inspection. Store credit does not expire. We cannot pay trade-in value as cash or to a bank account.

## Using trade-in credit

Store credit can be used on any product, but not on gift cards. It can be combined with discount codes. If you place an order before you send the trade-in, you can apply the quote later as a credit, up to 30 days after the order.

## If the value changes

If our inspection finds a different condition from the one you described, we send a new offer by email. You have 7 days to accept it. If you decline, we return the product to you free of charge.

## Recycling

Recycling is free for any audio product, whether or not it is a Lumen product. Batteries are removed and sent to a certified battery recycler, and the remaining metal and plastic are separated and recycled. We do not send products to landfill.

## Data on your product

Headphones and speakers can store pairing information and, for some models, voice assistant data. We wipe every product we receive. For your own peace of mind, you can reset the product before sending it.
