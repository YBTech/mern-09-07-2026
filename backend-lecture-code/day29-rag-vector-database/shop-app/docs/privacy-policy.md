# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Lumen Audio collects, why, and what choices you have.

## What we collect

We collect your name, email address, shipping address, and order history. Payment details are handled by our payment provider and never stored on our servers. If you contact support, we also keep the messages and any photos you send us.

## How we use your information

We use your information to process orders, ship parcels, answer support questions, and prevent fraud. If you opt in, we also use your email address to send offers. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your cart. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time from the link in the page footer.

## Marketing emails

We send marketing emails only if you opt in. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect within 2 business days. Order and shipping emails are not marketing, so you will still receive them.

## Sharing with other companies

We share only what is needed with our carriers, our payment provider, and our customer support software. These companies may use your information only to provide their service to us. We may also share information when a court or a law requires it.

## Payment information

Card numbers are entered into a form hosted by our payment provider and are never visible to Lumen Audio. We keep only the last four digits and the card type, so that we can show them on your order.

## Children

Our site is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## How long we keep data

Account information is kept while your account is open. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax reasons. Support messages are deleted after 2 years. Analytics data is anonymized after 14 months.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We reply within 30 days. To make a request, use the Privacy page in your account or email our privacy team.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the Account settings page. Personal data is removed within 30 days, except order records, which we keep for 7 years for tax reasons. Deleting your account also cancels unused loyalty points and discount codes.

## Data breaches

If a breach affects your personal information, we tell you by email within 72 hours of confirming it, explain what was exposed, and list the steps we recommend.
