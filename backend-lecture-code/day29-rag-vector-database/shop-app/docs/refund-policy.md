# Refund Policy

Lumen Audio sells headphones, speakers, and audio accessories online. We want you to be happy with what you buy, so we keep our return rules simple. This policy explains when an item can be returned, what it costs, and how and when you are refunded.

## Return window

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging, with the seal unbroken and every accessory included. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your tracking, not from the day you placed the order.

## How to start a return

Sign in, open the Orders page, choose the order, and select "Return an item". Pick the reason, and we email you a prepaid return label within one business day. Pack the item in its original box, attach the label, and drop the parcel at any carrier location. You do not need to call us first.

## Opened items

Opened headphones and speakers can be returned within 14 days of delivery. A 12% restocking fee is taken from the refund, because opened items must be cleaned, tested, and repackaged before they can be sold again. Ear tips, ear cushions, cables, and the charging case must be included. Items that show wear, scratches, or missing parts are not accepted.

## Restocking fee

The restocking fee is 12% of the item price, before tax and shipping. It applies only to opened items that work normally. It is never charged for items that are defective or damaged on arrival, for the wrong item, or for unopened items.

## Damaged or defective on arrival

If an item arrives broken, damaged, defective, or does not work out of the box, contact support within 7 days of delivery. We send a prepaid return label and give you a full refund, including the original shipping cost. No restocking fee applies. Photos of the damage and the shipping box speed up the process, and we may ask for a short video if a speaker or headphone will not power on.

## Wrong item received

If we sent the wrong model or color, tell us within 14 days of delivery. We ship the correct item first when it is in stock, and you return the wrong one with a prepaid label. You do not pay anything extra, and no restocking fee applies.

## Final-sale items

Items marked "Final sale" on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes clearance items, opened in-ear monitors and earphones for hygiene reasons, custom-engraved products, and gift cards. Final-sale items that arrive damaged or defective are still covered under the damaged or defective rule above.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, and wrong items. For any other return, a prepaid label costs $6.99, which is taken from your refund. You can also use your own carrier and keep the tracking number, in which case you pay the postage yourself and we refund only the item price.

## Exchanges

We do not swap items directly. To exchange a product, return the original for a refund and place a new order for the one you want. If you order first, the return window of the original item keeps running, so you do not lose your place.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is inspected at our warehouse. Orders paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit. Original shipping costs are refunded only for damaged, defective, or wrong items. Your bank may need a few extra days to show the refund on your statement.

## Returns without a receipt

You do not need a paper receipt. We find the order from your account or from the email address used at checkout. If an item was a gift, ask the buyer to start the return, or contact support, and we issue store credit to the recipient.

## Business and bulk orders

Orders of 10 or more units of the same item are treated as business orders. They can be returned within 14 days of delivery if unopened, but a 20% restocking fee applies to every unit. Contact our business team before sending a bulk return so we can arrange a freight pickup.
