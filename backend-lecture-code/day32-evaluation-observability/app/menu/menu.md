# Slice Pizza — Menu

All pizzas come in small (10"), medium (12") and large (16"). Prices are on the order screen.

## Margherita
San Marzano tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, basil and a drizzle of olive oil. Vegetarian.
Our best seller since we opened in 2019.

## Pepperoni
Tomato sauce, mozzarella and a double layer of cup-and-char pepperoni. The pepperoni is pork and beef.

## BBQ Chicken
Smoky barbecue sauce instead of tomato, grilled chicken thigh, red onion, mozzarella and coriander.
The barbecue sauce is made in house and contains soy and mustard.

## Veggie Garden
Basil pesto base, roasted peppers, mushrooms, red onion, black olives and mozzarella. Vegetarian.
The pesto is made with pine nuts and parmesan.

## Four Cheese
Mozzarella, gorgonzola, fontina and parmesan on a white garlic-cream base, no tomato. Vegetarian.
Parmesan is made with animal rennet, so strict vegetarians may want to skip it.

## Crust options
- Gluten-free crust: medium only, $3 extra. Made from rice and tapioca flour.
- Vegan cheese: any pizza, $2 extra. Cashew based.

## Drinks and sides
Coke, Sprite and sparkling water. Garlic knots come with a garlic-butter dip.
The Caesar salad uses a classic dressing with anchovy and raw egg yolk.
