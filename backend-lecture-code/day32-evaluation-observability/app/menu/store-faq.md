# Slice Pizza — Store FAQ

## Opening hours
Monday to Thursday 11 am to 10 pm. Friday and Saturday 11 am to midnight. Sunday noon to 9 pm.
The kitchen takes its last order 15 minutes before closing.

## Delivery area and fees
We deliver within 5 km of the shop at 88 Harbour Street. Delivery is $3.99, free on orders over $25.
Pickup orders are ready in about 20 minutes.

## Delivery time promise
We aim to deliver within 45 minutes. If your pizza takes more than 60 minutes from the time you
confirmed the order, the whole order is free: reply to your confirmation message and we refund it.

## Wrong or damaged order
Send a photo within 2 hours of delivery and we will remake it or refund the item, your choice.

## Payment
Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We take cash for pickup only. Your card is charged when you confirm
the order, not when you add items to the cart.

## Loyalty stamps
Every pizza earns one stamp. Ten stamps get a free large pizza of your choice. Stamps expire after 12 months.

## Large groups
For orders of more than 10 pizzas, please order at least 2 hours ahead so we can schedule the oven.
