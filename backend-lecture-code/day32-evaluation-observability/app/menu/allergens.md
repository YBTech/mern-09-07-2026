# Slice Pizza — Allergen Guide

We cook everything in one small kitchen. We list what is IN each item, but we cannot promise
that any item is free from traces of another allergen.

## Gluten
Every regular crust and the garlic knots contain wheat. The gluten-free crust is made on the same
counters and baked in the same oven as wheat dough, so it is suitable for a low-gluten diet but
NOT safe for people with coeliac disease.

## Tree nuts and peanuts
The Veggie Garden pesto contains pine nuts. The vegan cheese is made from cashews.
No item contains peanuts, but our pesto supplier also handles peanuts.
Customers with a nut allergy should avoid the Veggie Garden and the vegan cheese.

## Dairy
Every pizza has dairy cheese unless you choose vegan cheese. The garlic-butter dip contains butter.
The Four Cheese white base is made with cream.

## Soy and mustard
The BBQ Chicken barbecue sauce contains soy and mustard.

## Fish and egg
The Caesar salad dressing contains anchovy (fish) and raw egg. It is not recommended during pregnancy.

## Pork
The pepperoni contains pork. Ask for the BBQ Chicken if you avoid pork.
